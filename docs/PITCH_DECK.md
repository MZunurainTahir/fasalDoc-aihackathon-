## Slide 2: The Problem

### Pakistan's Farmers Are Losing Billions

- **30–40% annual crop loss** from diseases diagnosed too late
- **Rs. 800+ billion** yearly economic damage
- **1 extension officer for 2,000+ farmers** — help is physically unreachable
- **58M+ farming families** with no instant diagnostic tool
- **Livestock mortality** remains high due to delayed veterinary care

> "By the time the expert reaches the field, the disease has already spread."

## Slide 4: How It Works

1. Farmer opens FasalDoc PWA on any smartphone
2. Snaps a photo of affected crop or animal
3. AI identifies disease with confidence score
4. App displays organic + chemical remedies, dosage, cost, prevention
5. Farmer books a tele-vet, checks weather risk, mandi rates, yield forecasts, or community pest alerts
6. All data syncs to cloud when internet is available
